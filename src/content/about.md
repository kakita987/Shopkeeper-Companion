## About

Shopkeeper Companion started as a spreadsheet.

I created the original spreadsheet because I wanted a better way to track blueprint ownership, mastery progress, and crafting goals in Shop Titans. After using it for myself, I realized that if I found it useful, other players probably had the same need, so I shared a template with the community.

Over time, new blueprints continued to be released, and maintaining the spreadsheet became increasingly difficult. I wanted better ways to search, filter, and organize information while making it easier to keep up with new content.

That eventually led to building Shopkeeper Companion: a dedicated web app designed to make blueprint information easier to browse and manage.

### Built for Players

Shopkeeper Companion is designed to help players organize blueprint information and track their progress.

The app allows players to:
- Browse and search blueprint information
- Track ownership and mastery progress
- Create custom filtered views
- Sync their information with Google Sheets

### Community Driven

Shopkeeper Companion exists because of the Shop Titans community.

Blueprint information is based on community-maintained resources, and the app is designed to adapt as new content is released. Feedback, bug reports, and suggestions from players help guide future improvements.

### Independent Fan Project

Shopkeeper Companion is an independent fan project created by a Shop Titans player. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Kabam.

The app is free to use. If you choose to support the project through tips or donations, those contributions help cover hosting costs, domain costs, and continued development.
